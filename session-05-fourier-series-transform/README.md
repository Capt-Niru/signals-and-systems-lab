# Session 5 — Fourier Series and Fourier Transform

Verifying Fourier series expansions for periodic waves, examining convergence, and computing Fourier/inverse Fourier transforms with the Symbolic Math Toolbox.

## Covers
- Fourier series of a periodic triangular wave, truncated at N = 3, 5, 10
- Fourier series of a periodic square wave, truncated at N = 2, 5
- Exponential Fourier series of `f(t) = t, -1 < t < 1` and its line spectrum
- Fourier transform of a time-shifted step minus a decaying exponential
- Inverse Fourier transform of a given `X(omega)`
- Magnitude/phase (Bode-style) plot of a 4th-order transfer function in `s = j*omega`
